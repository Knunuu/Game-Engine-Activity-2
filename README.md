For this activity, I remade some mechanics from metroid.

The player can shoot 2 different projectiles, Bullets and Rockets.

The aiming follows the mouse. To shoot a regular bullet, press F, and to shoot a rocket, press G

Bullets deal lower damage, but are faster.
Rockets deal more damage, have AOE and can open stronger doors.

Doors can be opened by shooting them, blue doors can be opened from any projectile, while red ones can be opened by rockets.

The rocket inherits from the projectile class, but changing the reaction when hitting an enemy or wall, to make it do splash damage.
Projectiles grab variables from the door class, checking if its open and if its a strong door through functions.
